# Roadmap — MVP → full career-transition platform

The to-be state: an end-to-end companion from job loss through to being resettled in work — for individuals, employers running humane redundancy processes, and the professionals who support both. Not a job portal; a journey platform.

## Phase 0 — now (this MVP)
Guided journey (triage, calculator, checklist, glossary, directory, reset), AU + UK, journey map, monetisation framework wired but off, everything on-device.

**Exit criteria to launch:** both rulesets professionally reviewed (`governance.reviewer` filled), domain + hosting live, post-deploy checklist green.

## Phase 1 — credibility & reach (0–6 months)
- Content: articles/guides hub (also the natural home for Stream-1 ads)
- Privacy-respecting aggregate analytics (e.g. Plausible) — page counts only, never answers
- SEO fundamentals; grant applications begin (Stream 9)
- Streams 1–2 enabled once traffic justifies
- Quarterly legal recheck cadence operating

## Phase 2 — persistence & personalisation (6–12 months)
- Accounts + saved progress (auth provider; swap `lib/storage.js` for synced store)
- Personalised timeline & opt-in reminders ("your notice ends in 9 days") — derived from data the calculator already collects
- Region 3–4: Canada, NZ (data-only additions if they fit existing models)
- Stream 4 (sponsored placements) with the enforced free-first rule

## Phase 3 — the paid layer (12–18 months)
- Document vault (see SECURITY.md requirements) with rules-based "what's missing / looks off" checks per region
- Letter generator (raising underpayment, requesting written reasons)
- AI companion grounded in verified region content only
- Stream 3 (freemium) launches on the back of these

## Phase 4 — the career side (18–24 months)
- Resume rebuild assistant, interview reframing practice, payout-as-runway budgeting, skills-gap suggestions
- Moderated community (separate from advice; moderation tooling first)
- Marketplace of vetted professionals (Stream 5)

## Phase 5 — the business engine (24 months+)
- B2B employer compliance sibling (consultation checklists, notification tracking, audit trail) reusing the same region rulesets — Stream 6
- White-label licensing to outplacement firms and unions — Stream 7
- Structurally different regions (US at-will, EU civil-law) — new calculation models, not just data
- Interface translation (multi-language), aggregate insights reports (Stream 8, aggregate-only)

## Standing rules across all phases
1. Core rights info + calculator free forever.
2. No region ships unreviewed; no figure without source + date.
3. Data minimal, encrypted, deletable; each region's privacy regime assessed on its own.
4. Calm plain English; never a lead-gen funnel in disguise.
